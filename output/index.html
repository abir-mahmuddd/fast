Chairman of JH International

Introduction of the Chairman : The name of the chairman of JH Group is Mahbub Hossain. He is also known to many as Arafat. He was born in 1994 in a place called Nayarhat in the port area of Chattogram. He is currently 31 years old. At present, he is working at a Korean factory named Merimco Ltd. located in CEPZ. He has been working there for two years with experience as a Computer Helper in the Finishing Department.
Description of JH Group : Alongside his studies, Mahbub Hossain wanted to become an entrepreneur. He always thought about how to bring people together under a group and teach them something or do something together—be it good or bad. However, he couldn’t choose the right path. He ended up choosing the wrong one. He was always immersed in negative thoughts and ideologies. Eventually, he finished his studies and came to work at a Korean company called Merim Co. Ltd. It is a garment factory. Both men and women work there. He found some friends whose mentality matched his own. Working with the opposite gender, his desires started to overflow, and he turned into a JH Human. He began inviting more people to join his group. To fulfill his dream of becoming an entrepreneur, he established a group called JH International. He is working tirelessly from the grassroots level to make this group stronger.
